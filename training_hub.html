Training

Our approach ensures that each dog is trained to its full potential, building a strong relationship between dogs and their handlers. We have several obedience levels that cater to every level of experience. Our Obedience Training is for specialised breeds and includes Obedience, Socialising, Retrieve, Agility, Tracking and Protection Work. Training is important in a dog’s life because it improves character and confidence. We also believe that tranning dogs from a young age would be beneficial as thats when they can fully peak their potential
